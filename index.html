Why this system
atomic by design Atoms, molecules, and organisms with one job each — composed, never copy-pasted. light + dark Every component reads theme tokens, follows your OS setting, and remembers your toggle. accessible Native buttons, selects, and headings under the hood — AA contrast, visible focus, reduced motion. typed Built with TypeScript and Lit, so variants and props are checked, not guessed. no magic values Every color, space, and radius comes from a named token. CI fails raw hex or pixel literals. static-first Two static pages that deploy to GitHub Pages straight from a Bun + Vite build.
